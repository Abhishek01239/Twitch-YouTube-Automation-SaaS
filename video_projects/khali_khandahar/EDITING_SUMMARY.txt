THE EDIT (17:30 -> 10:55)
--------------------------
Structure
 1. Creator's disclaimer (kept — it's their safety notice).
 2. COLD OPEN hook (~11 s): four flashes from the night — running in the dark, the friend found
    in the corner, the panic, and the dropped phone in motion-interpolated slow motion — each
    hit with a sub-boom, then a riser into a black card with the creator's own logline:
    "They call it Khali Khandahar / because no living soul remains."
 3. Original opening credits + title (kept intact, credited cast/crew), braam on the title.
 4. CHAPTER 01 THE CHANNEL — temple vlog (subscribe pitch removed), walking speed-ramped 1.6x,
    room scene condensed from ~4 min to ~1.5 min.
 5. CHAPTER 02 THE ROAD — dusk walk cut to an 84 BPM pulse, speed ramps on wide walking shots,
    slow-motion feet-on-gravel beat, "is this the house?" moment, the old man confrontation,
    push-in on the building reveal with reverse swell + sub hit.
 6. CHAPTER 03 INSIDE — exploration tightened (dead air removed), heartbeat SFX as they split
    up, scares and the corner discovery kept almost whole.
 7. CHAPTER 04 THE WAY OUT — escape and climax kept nearly intact (it is the payoff), the
    creator's own strobe/glitch effects untouched, the final twist shot + title, end card.

Techniques
 - Cuts: chosen from the footage, then snapped to the quietest half-second within +/-0.75 s so
   no word is chopped; 20-30 ms audio fades on every cut (no clicks).
 - Jump cuts inside long takes are hidden with alternating subtle push-ins / pull-outs
   (4-10%); no flashy transitions — hard cuts, a few dips to black between acts, whooshes
   only on chapter changes.
 - Speed ramps 1.25-1.6x on walking; slow motion 0.5-0.6x with motion-compensated frame
   interpolation (no stutter).
 - Colour grade per scene: day (warm, gently desaturated), room (tamed magenta walls, cooler),
   dusk (teal shadows / warm highlights), night (cold, desaturated, slightly lifted shadows);
   vignette + fine film grain over everything for a consistent cinematic look.
 - Motion graphics: chapter lower-thirds that slide in, title cards with slow tracking.
 - Captions: animated pop-in English subtitles ONLY for lines whose meaning was clear in the
   Whisper transcript (e.g. "Kids, what are you doing here?", "Is Sumant here?",
   "Don't leave him alone, okay?"). The Hindi transcript was too unreliable for verbatim
   captions everywhere, so unclear dialogue was left uncaptioned rather than invented.

AUDIO
-----
 - Dialogue (v2): the raw dialogue of the 33 speaking clips (9:41) was cleaned with the
   ElevenLabs Voice Isolator (9,677 credits); 25% of a noisereduce-de-noised track is blended
   back so the horror ambience isn't stripped. Then: 90 Hz high-pass, -3 dB at 250 Hz (mud),
   +4 dB at 3.2 kHz (presence), de-esser, compression. Clips not sent to the isolator (music-led
   speed ramps, credits) use the noisereduce track. Lip sync verified: 22-25 ms (< 1 frame).
 - Music: three ORIGINAL beds synthesized from scratch in Python (no third-party music, so no
   copyright risk): dark ambient drone, 84 BPM dusk pulse, 112 BPM heartbeat climax bed.
   Music is side-chain ducked under dialogue automatically.
 - SFX (also synthesized): riser, braam impact, sub hit, whoosh, static glitch, heartbeat,
   reverse swell — placed on the hook, title, reveals and scares.
 - Master: limiter + EBU R128 loudness normalisation to -14 LUFS (YouTube's target).
